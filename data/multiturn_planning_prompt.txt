Your task is to create a comprehensive writing plan for this prompt.

Your plan should be thorough and follow this exact format:

# Brainstorming

Brainstorm ideas for characters, plot, tone, story beats, and pacing over the {n_chapters} chapters. The purpose of brainstorming is to cast a wide net of ideas, not to settle on any specific direction. Think about various ways you could take the prompt. Consider:
- Multiple potential character archetypes and their motivations
- Different plot structures and narrative arcs
- Tonal choices (dark, humorous, melancholic, hopeful, etc.)
- Possible themes and subtext
- Story beats and their placement across chapters

# Reflection

Reflect on what works and doesn't work in these ideas. The purpose of this reflection is to narrow in on what you think will work best to make a piece that is: a) compelling, and b) fits the prompt requirements. Consider trade-offs between different approaches.

# Chapter-by-Chapter Plan

Write a detailed plan for each chapter:

## Chapter 1
- Main events/scenes
- Character development goals
- Emotional arc for this chapter
- Key imagery or sensory details to include
- How it sets up subsequent chapters

# Character Profiles

For each major character (at least 2-3 characters), provide:

## [Character Name]
- Background: Brief backstory and current situation
- Personality: Key traits, quirks, speech patterns
- Motivation: What drives them in this story
- Arc: How they change over the course of the narrative
- Voice: How they speak, think, and perceive the world (include a sample line of dialogue)
